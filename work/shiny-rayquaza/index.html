08 · Stylized and fan art

Shiny Rayquaza

Fan art. Pokémon © Nintendo / Game Freak.

Stylized character piece — Shiny Rayquaza fan art. Sculpted and modeled in Blender with a hand-painted, toon-leaning material setup and rim lighting to keep the silhouette readable.

Includes a 12-second animation. 47 objects, ~1.8k base polygons, all driven by mirror + subdivision. Clay and wireframe included — the sparkles are modeled geometry, not a post effect.
