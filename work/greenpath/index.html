Greenpath — Hollow Knight

Fan art. Hollow Knight © Team Cherry.

Stylized environment from an in-progress animated short based on Hollow Knight. Modular rock and foliage kit, atmospheric depth built with volumetrics, and a restricted green palette so the characters stay readable against a busy background.

Everything modeled, shaded, lit and rendered by me in Blender.
