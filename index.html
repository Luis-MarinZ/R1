Product visualization

  1. 02

    The Sun Cola — PET Bottle

    Beverage product visualization for a cola brand concept. The hard part here isn't the bottle — it's stacking transparent PET over a dark liquid, with condensation on top, and still keeping the label readable and the brand colors clean.

  2. 03

    Tsunami Hot Sauce

    Product render for a hot sauce brand concept. Single-hue lighting study — everything is driven by one warm key and a controlled falloff, so the glass, the sauce and the peppers read as separate materials without breaking the monochrome mood.

  3. 04

    Blue Bell — Eau de Parfum

    Studio product visualization for a perfume concept. The brief I set myself: sell a fully transparent object with no color to rely on — so the whole shot rests on the blind-shadow pattern, the glass thickness and the contact shadow.

  4. 05

    Concept Laptop

    Tech product visualization. Moody, low-key lighting with colored rim light and a reflective floor — the kind of hero shot used for launch banners and landing pages.

  5. 06

    The Otisense — Serum Packaging

    Packaging and campaign concept for a skincare/pharma serum. Dropper bottle modeled from scratch, amber glass shading, and a floating particle system standing in for the active formula.

  6. 07

    Border — Headphones

    Consumer product visualization. Focus on soft-goods shading — the fabric ear cushions and matte plastic had to read as clearly different materials on a neutral background, with no scene to hide behind.

About

3D artist specializing in product and packaging visualization, with a Graphic Design background. I turn products and brands into photoreal and stylized renders — beverages, cosmetics, apparel and consumer goods — owning the full pipeline in Blender: modeling, materials, lighting, rendering and compositing.

My design foundation means I don't just render a product, I art-direct it: composition, color and brand storytelling come first.

Based in Oklahoma, USA (CST). Authorized to work in the U.S. Open to remote, hybrid and on-site roles, plus freelance projects.
